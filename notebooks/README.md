1. `RQ1_00_Build_Once` - segment the corpus five ways and embed it once, cached on Drive

4. `RQ1_E_Answer_RQ1` - main effects and the answer to RQ1
5. `RQ2_0_Segmentation_Ceiling` - the ceiling that decides S1
6. `RQ2_Stage_Attribution` - stage partition, decomposition and the answer to RQ2
7. `RQ3_Certainty_Calibration` - certainty score, calibration, citation test and the answer to RQ3

Each notebook ends with an answer section and worked examples.
